# Lost Centuries interludes

## Interlude A (chapters lc01 to lc03, ids `lc01`, `lc02`, `lc03`)

Chapter 20 now sleeps only the first stretch (`rome_to_sack`). The interludes wake the hero in the sack of 410, then in the Emperor's new city in 537, and each ends by lying down again.

lc01, "The Salarian Gate" (410 AD). Woken by the night the Goths take Rome. The hero feeds, learns the news, meets the circle, then spends the night across four places: the house (flag `lc.collis`), the gate (`lc.gate`), the court (`lc.court`) and the hill (`lc.hill`). Dawn closes it.

lc02, "The Third Day". The road out and the new seat: `lc.road`, `lc.watchers`, `lc.seat`. Rest place, steward, orders and protection are chosen again. Ends with `sleep("sack_to_byzantium")` (fall of the west). Chapter journal in the voice of Rufinus of the Aventine watch.

lc03, "The Second Rome" (537 AD). Woken in Constantinople. Flags: `lc.answer`, `lc.sunwalk`, `lc.hill_537`, `lc.court_537`, `lc.mason`. The cistern court, the dedication of the Great Church (collection item `great_church_tessera`). Ends with `sleep("byzantium_to_plague")`. Chapter journal in the voice of a litter bearer at the western door.

## Interlude B (chapters lc04 and lc05)

lc04, "The Year of the Pestilence" (spring 1348). Woken at Collesanto as the plague reaches the valley; the Valerii keepers or the bank's agent do the waking. Two of three night places: the village houses (`lc.village_1348`), the chapel where the Sleeper cult has grown (`lc.sleeper_1348`: denied, allowed, embraced), the north road (`lc.plague_road`). Dawn closes it.

lc05, "The Last Lying Down". Place, steward (bank, or keepers if sworn), orders and protection a last time, then `sleep("plague_to_florence")`, which ends Book One. Sleeper cult seed from interlude A is now the village saint.
