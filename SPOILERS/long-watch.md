# The Long Watch (interludes at the start of Unchained)

Unnumbered interlude chapters in `content/long-watch/`, listed first in the Unchained manifest entry. The hero stays awake from here on, so there are no sleeps. Each chapter has a Velati journal in `content/journal/`. Session 72 covers 1937 to the 1960s; session 73 covers the 1980s to the 2000s (lw04 to lw06).

## lw01, "The Bank on Harrison Street" (Chicago, spring 1937)
Opening: the business lead's report (or Dora Valer's, with no coven) on the new hospital blood bank, then the village letter (`lw.hill_1937`: built shelters or lamps, sent coin, waited). Hunger scene offers the bank, a donor or a stranger. Night, two of three: the cold room (`lw.supply`, a loop; public drive, paid donors, a private cold store under the Yards; most committed wins), the public door (`lw.door`: a gift holder with charm, the lead, himself), the ledger (own none, own the bottle makers' share, own the bank through a charity). Dawn.

## lw02, "The Valley at War" (Collesanto, autumn 1943)
Opening: the hill in the war, the lead at the gate; reads `lw.hill_1937`. Night, two of three: the cellars (`lw.shelter`, a loop counted by picks: food, the crypt, the mule road), the villa (`lw.villa`: faced the major, sold him a ghost story, paid him), the gate (`lw.gate`: family or himself; a shadows or strength gift holder, or the lead, stands it). Dawn on the wall with the lead.

## lw03, "The Leads" (the 1960s)
Opening: the cabinet of folders, the lead keeping the files; reads `original(...)` states. Night, two of three leads (`lw.leads`: none, one or two): Baghdad (the groove of a chain in a dig's floor; a memory gift holder recognizes it), Geneva (a box paid for every January; a card inside), Vienna (a Velati reading room; the book of the watch). Dawn: who keeps the watch (`lw.keep`: coven, Collis or the Valer keepers).

Lore notes (none change the big picture): the groove of a chain at the Baghdad dig and the Geneva card ("Not yet. Be patient. I am coming to you.") are hooks for Book Seven and are not assigned to any original. Change their source freely when the Book Seven plan needs it.

Hooks left for session 73: the 1980s Collis on Wall Street; the card's sender; the Velati's answer to the letter; the groove under the dig; what each keeper line has filed.

## lw04, "The Floor" (New York, October 1987)
Opening: the lead's report from the thirty ninth floor. Night, two of three: the floor (`lw.crash`, a loop: sold, held and lent, bought; a merchants eye holder reads the tape), the keys (`lw.haven`, a loop: brownstone, warehouse, tower), the card (`lw.rival`: dinner with Maude Hollis of the Hollis house, the second oldest coven in the country; refused, pact, or warned that the house is the sixth blood).

## lw05, "The Answer" (the 1990s)
Opening: three envelopes; reads `lw.keep`. Night, two of three: the Swiss letter (`lw.card`: alone to the flat with the green door, with the family, or refused; the sender is not there), the Order's answer (`lw.order`: audience, family, refused; the Order is split on the sixth blood), the machine (`lw.net`, a loop: back a young founder, put the house's books on it, own the wires). Dawn reads `lw.rival`.

## lw06, "The Last Ordinary Year" (2008)
Opening: the family gathered in the haven chosen in lw04. Night, two of three: the bank on Hudson Street (`lw.bailout`: lent, stood quietly behind, let fall; reads `lw.crash`), the Hollis house in ruin (`lw.fall`: took in, bought, left), the call from Geneva (`lw.ready`: tell the family, tell no one, call back). Dawn: "the family is in the house."

Lore notes (none change the big picture): the Hollis house is a rival coven of another line, not of the sixth blood; it can return in Book Seven as ally, rival or ghost. The Geneva voice says it is time and will send for him once; its sender is left unnamed and is not assigned to any original. The Order is divided into those who fear and those who would keep the sixth blood. Change any of these freely when the Book Seven plan needs it.
